- AAPL: compatible annual EPS and forward growth exist; no defensible target P/E. Enterprise claims and WACC/NWC are missing. Common-book and dividend frameworks lack verified scope/suitability.
- MSFT: compatible EPS forecast exists, but target P/E is missing. Complete debt and aggregate D&A mappings are additionally unavailable. A partial long-term-debt balance is not total debt.
- NVDA: extraordinary historical growth and incompatible split-era EPS prevent automatic growth forecasts. No target multiple or complete enterprise claims. Verified split events and as-of reporting vintages are required.
- ORCL: issuer share-class basis and common-income/EPS numerator do not reconcile; no permitted market quote/history. Explicit quoted-class forecasts remain user assumptions. No quote or share-class conversion may be manufactured.

## Historical evidence constraints

Normalized Phase 3 periods select later comparative/restated filings. Reusing their EPS with earlier prices would introduce look-ahead bias. Historical analytics must select original as-of SEC filing vintages, reject unknown price adjustment bases, account for verified splits, distinguish trailing from forward-proxy multiples, and use at least three independent fiscal-year samples. Today's quote cannot determine an intrinsic target multiple. Absent concepts are never verified zeros. Current DEI shares must retain their actual observation date and class scope; annual diluted weighted shares remain separate.

## Implementation boundary

Automatic evidence is additive and optional. Provider failures yield source-specific unavailable records; they do not copy Demo data, suppress independent models, or weaken Phase 4 validation. User overrides and reset defaults stay transient. Historical multiple/forecast/rate methods and final actual-company results are documented in the Phase 4.5 implementation report after verification. No automatic commit, push, OpenAI integration or Phase 5 work is authorized.
